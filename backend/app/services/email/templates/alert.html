What this means
Smoke at this level can affect people with heart or lung disease (including asthma), older adults, children, pregnant people and people who work or exercise outdoors. Some values above may be below the official "Unhealthy for Sensitive Groups" level (35.5 µg/m³). The model tends to under-predict heavy smoke, so we alert at a lower value for longer forecasts.

What you can do

  • Check current conditions before spending time outside.
  • Cut back on hard or long outdoor activity.
  • Keep windows and doors closed when it smells smoky. Use an air purifier or set your AC to recirculate if you have one.
  • If you have asthma or heart or lung disease, keep your medicine close and follow your care plan.
